# Step 5 Workflow: Accept Or Reject Candidate

## Objective

Prevent failed or inconclusive candidates from being counted as improvement.

## Required actions

1. Accept only evidence-supported improvements.
2. For matrix-backed comparisons, base acceptance on comparable prior and candidate entries, not on unmatched matrix rows.
3. Archive, revise, or report regressed candidates honestly.
4. Separate strict composite increase from scorer-specific gains, metric-specific gains, and secondary improvements such as lower duration or fewer tokens.

## Done when

- Candidate status is explicit.
- Scorer, metric, quality, composite, and efficiency evidence supports the status.
- Matrix-backed acceptance references the matched entries that support the decision.
- No failed candidate is counted as a successful revision.
